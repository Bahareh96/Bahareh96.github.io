EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH

From the wind tunnel to structural performance.

At Florida International University’s NHERI Wall of Wind facility, I conducted experiments on a 1:76-scale aeroelastic tall-building model, investigating building interference and passive and active façade configurations using acceleration, pressure, and aerodynamic load measurements.

Research projects

2026 · NHERI Wall of Wind, Florida International University

Large-scale aeroelastic testing & building interference

Conducted wind tunnel experiments on a 1:76-scale aeroelastic tall-building model to investigate how neighboring buildings and aerodynamic modifications affect structural response. The experimental program included baseline, interference, and passive and active façade configurations under varying wind conditions.

Acceleration, pressure, and aerodynamic load measurements support comparisons of structural response and wind loading across configurations.

2023–2026 · Iowa State University

AI-driven adaptive vibration control

Developed a cyber-physical dynamic façade system using artificial intelligence and optimization algorithms for adaptive wind-induced vibration control. Evaluated the system through large-scale aeroelastic wind tunnel experiments.

This work connects sensing, computational control, and actuated aerodynamic modifications to study real-time structural response mitigation.

2025 · Iowa State University

Sustainable structural optimization

Applied optimization techniques to structural model analysis to reduce material use and environmental impacts while maintaining safety and performance.

Related journal work addresses optimized steel structural systems for reduced carbon footprint and enhanced resiliency in wind.

2024 · Iowa State University

Performance-based wind design

Developed a finite element model and evaluated building response under extreme wind events to support performance-based design.

Related work explores database-assisted design approaches for evaluating the performance of high-rise buildings.

2023 · Iowa State University

Windborne debris impact analysis

Developed a finite element model to assess debris impacts on building façades and inform protective design strategies.

Related published research examines aluminum foam structural wall panels under single and repeated windborne debris impacts.

2018–2021 · Amirkabir University of Technology

Cyclic response of structural shear walls

Developed a finite element model to predict shear-wall response under repeated lateral loading.

Conference work includes cyclic modeling of cold-formed steel shear walls and the application of concrete-filled chord studs.
